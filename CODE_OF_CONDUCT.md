# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Members, contributors, and maintainers of the Thoth community pledge to
make participation a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, level of experience, or perspective.

We commit to interactions that build the project up — through clear
disagreement when warranted, generous interpretation when intent is
unclear, and acknowledgement of contribution at every level.

## Expected behavior

- Respect differing viewpoints; argue ideas, not people
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Acknowledge mistakes and learn from them
- Center the well-being of the community over individual preference
- Use clear, professional language

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Trolling, sustained derailment of discussions
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — repository, issue tracker,
Discord, social media accounts, conferences, and any other venue where
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report violations to **conduct@thoth-runtime.dev**. Reports are reviewed
within 48 hours by the project maintainers. Reporter identity is kept
confidential to the extent permitted by investigation needs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers determine the consequences of any violation. Possible actions:

1. **Correction.** Private clarification, request for behavior change.
2. **Warning.** Public written warning; explanation of which behaviors are
   unacceptable.
3. **Temporary ban.** Time-limited removal from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban.** Removal from all project spaces.

Determination is at the maintainers' discretion based on severity and
pattern of behavior.
